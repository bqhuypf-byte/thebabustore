# Product content workflow

For product descriptions, technical specifications, SEO metadata, and product editorial work, read `skills/product_description_seo/SKILL.md` and `docs/product-description-seo-workflow.md` before editing.

The user's current scope is products with at least one variant whose stock is exactly `in_stock`. Recheck stock immediately before applying bulk revisions. Leave out-of-stock product records unchanged unless a later user request explicitly expands the scope.

Keep one shared product template, Roboto typography, and editable structured content in admin. Do not restore customer-visible SKU, the removed purchase-area summary/contact block, or the removed version-selection hint. Keep original descriptions and images available for editing. Never fabricate specifications, prices, stock, warranties, or reviews.

This file does not require a separate approval step for work already authorized by the user.
